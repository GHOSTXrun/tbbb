Privacy technology

Confidential by Design. Precise about the rest.

Helius Privacy Rings keep balances and transfer amounts as encrypted notes on Solana. Zero-knowledge proofs — generated by an attested enclave prover — let the program verify every transfer without seeing its contents.

In the Default Ring, who sent to whom stays public. NULL shows you, on every screen, which fields are confidential and which are not.
