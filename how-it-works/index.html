Helius Privacy Rings

Solana Privacy Rings is a permissionless privacy protocol by Helius, live on Solana mainnet. Instead of a visible token balance, you hold encrypted notes in a shared pool. Each note records an asset and amount that only its owner can decrypt.

Spending a note requires a zero-knowledge proof that you own it and that inputs equal outputs, without revealing either. The on-chain program checks the proof; nobody else learns the amounts. Proofs are generated by a remote prover running inside an attested AWS Nitro enclave. The SDK verifies the enclave's attestation against a policy signed by Helius, then encrypts every proof request to it. NULL's servers relay those requests but only ever see ciphertext.

The protocol has been audited by Certora and zkSecurity (scope: the Solana Privacy Program and circuits). A third audit is listed by Helius as awaiting report. NULL uses the official TypeScript SDK, @heliuslabs/spr, which is still an alpha release.

What is hidden, and what is not

NULL uses the Default Ring, which is confidential, not anonymous. Amounts and assets inside the ring are encrypted. The addresses that take part are not. Every screen in NULL marks each value as ○ Public or ● Confidential.

The Vault key

Your protected balance belongs to a Vault identity: a key generated in your browser, encrypted with your passphrase (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds, then AES-256-GCM) and stored only on your device. Your normal Solana wallet stays your wallet. It pays network fees and signs deposits, and NULL never asks for its seed phrase.

Privacy Rings can also derive keys from a wallet signature. Helius tells wallets to refuse that request from third-party sites, because the signature unlocks your private history. NULL follows that advice and keeps the Vault key separate. You hold it as an encrypted backup file plus your passphrase.

The four tools

01 · Private Launch

Your wallet creates the token on pump.fun with pump.fun's official SDK. A second transaction then creates the mint's Privacy Rings SPL interface, so anyone can shield it. The two steps are separate transactions, each with its own status, because pump.fun's create transaction is already close to Solana's size limits.

02 · Private Vault

The Vault lets you shield (deposit), send privately (ring to ring), unshield (withdraw to any address), split a note into equal parts, and merge small notes. Your balance and history are decrypted locally from the Helius indexer, and NULL stores none of it.

03 · Stealth Trade

Stealth Trade creates trading wallets in your browser and funds them from your Vault with an unshield. You then trade through Jupiter, with each swap signed locally. When you are done, you shield the proceeds back into the Vault. Your main wallet never appears in the trades. The edges of the ring, meaning deposits and withdrawals, remain visible.

04 · Sealed P2P

Offer terms are encrypted with a key that lives only in the share link's #fragment. Both legs settle in one Solana transaction built only from the System and SPL Token programs. Solana executes a transaction completely or not at all, so neither side can receive without paying. Before any funds move, the seller pre-signs against a durable nonce. The buyer verifies the transaction byte-for-byte against the terms, then co-signs.

Limits, stated plainly

  • Nothing here makes activity anonymous or untraceable.
  • Deposit and withdrawal amounts are public. So are timing, fee payers and the link between a Vault identity and the wallets that fund it. A patient observer can correlate them.
  • Anonymous rings, which also hide sender and recipient, are not live on mainnet yet. NULL does not claim their properties.
  • Losing your backup file or passphrase means losing access to your protected balance. Nobody, including NULL, can recover it.
  • The Privacy Rings SDK is alpha software, so use amounts you can afford to have stuck while issues are fixed.
